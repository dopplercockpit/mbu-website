The Making of "Magnificent Michigan" Epoxy Table & Metal Bridge

🎥 Watch VIDEOS of the Build: @OldLadyWoodworking

This one-of-a-kind hand-craftsmanship and construction make this piece the epitome of functional ART. Both its remarkable natural shape, that strikingly resembles Michigan's Lower Peninsula and its large percentage of live edges make this white oak slab a rare find and truly a unique piece of ART! Connecting the upper and lower peninsulas is a metal replica of the Mackinaw Bridge with over 1,000 welds, 1,500 cuts and 3,000 grinds. Lakes of majestic blue epoxy resin (25 gallons) unify the live edges of this truly unique and inimitable piece.

Magnificent Michigan Table

Michigan's upper and lower peninsulas are a stately white oak and the surrounding land areas are a deep rich walnut, all native to Michigan. This table seats 14 people.
