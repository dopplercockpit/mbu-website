About the Book

The Making of Magnificent Michigan is a stunning coffee table book that chronicles the incredible journey of creating a one-of-a-kind epoxy river table shaped like Michigan's Lower Peninsula.

This table seats up to 14 people and measures 10 ft by 6 ft by 2 inches deep. The white oak slab's natural shape strikingly resembles Michigan's Lower Peninsula, with a large percentage of live edges making it a rare find.

Follow along as artist Dana Greene transforms this raw slab into a functional work of art, combining traditional woodworking with modern epoxy techniques to create the Magnificent Michigan table.

Features:

  • ✓ Full-color photography
  • ✓ Step-by-step build process
  • ✓ Behind-the-scenes insights
  • ✓ Perfect for woodworking enthusiasts
